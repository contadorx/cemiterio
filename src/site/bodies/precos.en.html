Why the free plan has no catch

It is a fair question: a free tool usually charges somewhere else — data, ads, or a limit that shows up at the worst moment. Here the reason is simpler: your video is processed by your own computer, so the cost of serving you is close to zero. There is nothing to subsidise, and therefore nothing to recover later.
